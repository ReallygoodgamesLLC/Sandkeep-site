Support

Need help with Sandkeep? Email reallygoodgamesllc@gmail.com and we will get back to you, usually within a couple of days. Please include your iPhone model and what happened.

Restore my purchases

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases, signed in to the same Apple ID you bought with. Keeps, troop outfits, banners, bundles, the Season Pass and Sandkeep Plus come back. Boosts that were already used do not.

Move my progress to a new iPhone

Sign in to Game Center (or Apple or Google in Settings) on the new iPhone with the same account. Your cloud save loads automatically.

Cancel Sandkeep Plus

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Sandkeep Plus. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.

Multiplayer will not connect

Both players need the latest version and an internet connection. The host should keep the game open on screen until the other player joins.

Delete my account and data

In the game, open Settings and tap Delete Account and Online Data. You can also email us and we will delete it for you.
