A real-time castle strategy game

Raise a fortress in the burning sands, train an army and break the walls of six rival clans. Every soldier, every wall and every arrow counts.

  • 100 campaign missions across five acts, plus Hard mode
  • Siege engines: rams, catapults and fire ballistas
  • Survival, the Daily Challenge, skirmishes and seasonal events
  • Online co-op and head-to-head with a friend
  • Map Maker: paint your own desert and share it
  • Keeps and troops with bonuses you can upgrade, and a Season Pass
