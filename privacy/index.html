Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy explains what Sandkeep, made by Kenneth Morff (“we”, “us”), collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Account: you are signed in automatically with Game Center, or as a guest, and you can also sign in with Apple or Google. Google Firebase gives you an anonymous player ID that links your progress to that sign-in. We never receive your password.

Game progress: stars, achievements, renown, the items you own, your settings and your saved game are stored on your device and in our cloud save, so you can carry on playing on another device.

Leaderboards, shared maps and multiplayer: your display name, scores and the maps you choose to share are visible to other players. In multiplayer, your moves and quick chat messages are sent to the other player.

Purchases: Apple or Google process every payment, and we use RevenueCat to check which items and subscriptions you have bought. We never receive your card details.

Ads

If you have not subscribed to Sandkeep Plus, ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect your device’s advertising identifier, approximate location and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. On iPhone you are asked for permission before your advertising identifier is used for tracking, and players in the European Economic Area and the UK are asked for consent first. See Google’s privacy policy for how Google uses this data.

Services we use

Google Firebase (sign-in, cloud save, leaderboards, shared maps and multiplayer), RevenueCat (purchases) and Google AdMob (ads). Each handles data under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep data

Your cloud save, scores and shared maps are kept until you delete your account. Multiplayer match data is temporary and removed after the match.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can reset your campaign progress in Settings, and delete your account and online data at any time with Delete Account and Online Data in Settings. You can turn off ad tracking in your iPhone settings, and players in the EEA and UK can change their ad choices with Ad Privacy Choices in Settings. You can also email us to ask for a copy of your data or to have it deleted.

Changes

We may update this policy, and will show the new date above.
